yt-studio

A private publishing tool for our own YouTube channels.

yt-studio is an internal tool operated by VersiyonBir. It produces videos for the YouTube channels we own and manage, uploads them to those channels, schedules their publication, keeps their titles, descriptions and thumbnails up to date, and reads their basic statistics.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the channel owners we work with connect their YouTube accounts to it, through Google's standard OAuth consent screen.
